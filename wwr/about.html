History

Our purpose is creating enticing memories for individuals and groups. Our mission is to extend rafting as an acessible activity for anyone in the south. Out motto is: See the water bubble like a good bottle os sparkling water.

Our company started in 2022 after the grim period of Covid settled down.

We had the intent to bring back joy and fun while also paying tribute and remembering all those who can not be with us.
